PILLAR I • CURATION

The Art of Discerning Selection

In an era dominated by mass industrialization, Oryx champions the rare, the intricate, and the soulful. We do not merely stock timepieces; we curate legacies. Each mechanical creation admitted into our vault represents hundreds of hours of manual finishing—from Geneva striping and mirror anglage to hand-blued screws.

Single-Source Provenance: Direct allocations from master artisans and verified Swiss manufactures with intact documentation.
Complication Specialists: Focused on tourbillons, perpetual calendars, minute repeaters, and astronomical displays.
PILLAR II • RIGOR

48-Point Chronometric Inspection

Before a timepiece is released to a collector, it undergoes an exhaustive evaluation by our certified master watchmakers. We inspect escapement lubrication, measure amplitude across six isobaric positions, and verify water resistance under simulated hyperbaric pressure.

Isochronism Verification: Multi-position rate consistency measured continuously over 120 hours.
Laser Micro-Casing Analysis: Flawless crystal and bezel surface integrity inspection under 40x magnification.
